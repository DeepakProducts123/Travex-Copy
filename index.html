Room Occupancy Fundamentals — A Beginner's Guide

Learn the foundational rules governing how hotel contracts translate into extranet room parameters.

Rule 01

Base vs. Maximum Occupancy

Base Occupancy is the number of guests included in the standard room rate (typically 2 adults).

Maximum Capacity is the absolute ceiling under the hotel fire code and contract. An extra guest is permitted only if explicitly configured.

Base tells you the base setup; Max tells you the ceiling.
Rule 02

Combinations ≠ Total Guest Count

A room with Total Occupancy = 4 does NOT automatically permit every combination of 4 guests.

2 Adults + 2 Children may be permitted, whereas 4 Adults or 3 Adults + 1 Child may be strictly prohibited by contract.

Validate the exact adult/child mix, not just the sum!
Rule 03

Extra Occupancy vs. Extra Bed

Extra Occupancy: A contractual permission allowing an additional person into the room.

Extra Bed: A physical rollaway or sofa bed arrangement requiring physical floor space and separate supplements.

Permission to stay ≠ provision of an extra physical bed!
Rule 04

Age-Based Occupancy & Sharing

Child Sharing: Child shares existing bedding with adults (subject to age limit and charge policy).

Free Child: Free accommodation does not guarantee free meals or free extra beds.

Always verify age bands, meal policies, and bed requirements.

CRITICAL BUG CASE STUDY

Production Defect: Two-Bedroom Suite Accepting 5 Adults + 1 Child

Detailed analysis of the reported extranet occupancy flaw and its business impact.

Current System Flaw

The Defect

A customer searches for 5 Adults + 1 Child (Age 6). The system evaluates: Total = 6 guests. Because the 2-Bedroom Suite has a nominal total capacity of 6, the system accepts the booking and quotes a rate.

Reality: The hotel contract strictly permits a maximum of 4 Adults + 2 Children, or 4 Adults + 1 Extra Bed. 5 adults are never permitted in this suite category!
Expected Extranet Behaviour

Contract Enforcement

The system must validate the exact guest tuple: (Adults: 5, Children: 1) against permitted configuration matrices.

Because the maximum adult cap is 4, the search must be rejected or suppressed from the search results, preventing an unfulfillable booking at the front desk.

Rule: Search.Adults (5) > Contract.MaxAdults (4) → INVALID_COMBINATION

Child Policies, Age Bands, and Bed Arrangements

Detailed breakdown of how age bands, free-child allowances, and physical bedding interact.

🛏

Child Bedding Modalities

  • Sharing Existing Bed: Child sleeps in the parent's King or Twin bed. Requires no extra furniture. May be free or have a nominal fee.
  • With Extra Bed: Child occupies a separate rollaway bed. Consumes 1 extra bed capacity and incurs an extra-bed surcharge.
  • Without Extra Bed: Explicit contract term stating no physical rollaway will be provided, even if an extra occupancy supplement is paid.
👶

Free-Child Quantity vs. Age Bands

  • Age 0–2.99 (Infant): Free of charge in existing bedding or complimentary baby cot (subject to availability).
  • Age 3–5.99 (Child 1 Free): First child stays free of room charges when sharing existing bedding.
  • Age 6–11.99 (Child Chargeable): Incurs contracted child supplement (e.g. AED 75/night).
  • Age 12+ (Adult Rate): Evaluated strictly as an adult guest for occupancy and pricing.
🍽

Accommodation vs. Meal Charge Rule

Crucial Rule: Free accommodation does NOT mean free meals.

Even when Child 1 is granted AED 0 room accommodation under the free-child policy, lunch/dinner or compulsory Gala Dinner supplements must still be billed at the contracted child meal rate!

Never assume meals are complimentary because room stays are free.

Key Rules for Seasonal Rate Loading

1. No Date Overlaps: The system must reject configurations where two different base rates exist for the exact same night in the same room.
2. Detect Calendar Gaps: If dates 01–04 Dec are missing, the system must not guess previous season prices; it must return a clear setup error.
3. Boundary Precision: Checkout date is not charged for that night's accommodation; rate is billed on check-in date nights only.

Questions to Clarify with the Product Owner (Slide 20)

Every requirement must be categorized clearly as Confirmed Issue, Assumption, or Open Question.

Classification Contract / System Question Current System State Agreed Resolution / Next Step
CONFIRMED ISSUE Reported 2BR case accepts 5 adults + 1 child, which hotel contract strictly denies. Accepted because total guests (6) ≤ nominal capacity (6). Add exact adult ceiling validator to suppress unsupported offer.
OPEN QUESTION Is extra-bed quantity fixed per room or per occupancy combination? Room occupancy screen has 1 global extra occupancy field. Clarify with PO: can different combinations have varying extra-bed rules?
OPEN QUESTION Can one guest combination be offered simultaneously with and without an extra bed? Current UI has single toggle. Determine whether to present guest two rate options at checkout.
ASSUMPTION "Extra Occupancy" in the UI means guest permission, not necessarily extra bedding. Currently ambiguous. Confirmed via documentation; physical bed requires separate supplement.
OPEN QUESTION Can two children share existing beds? Which exact age bands apply? Age dropdowns currently lack hotel-specific cutoffs. Define contract age limits (e.g. 0-2 infant, 3-5 free, 6-11 chargeable).
OPEN QUESTION Are adult and child meal supplements bundled or charged separately? Extranet displays combined rate plans. Allow itemized meal supplements (RO, BB, HB) in pricing section.
